RESTAURANT MENU & ORDERING INTERFACE DESIGN


TASK 1

Task: Design a digital restaurant menu and ordering interface that allows
customers to browse items by category, view food images, and place orders easily
(for dine-in or takeaway).

🔍 What I worked on:
* Designed the end-to-end user flow starting from QR scan to order completion
* Created a category-based digital menu (All, Starters, Mains, Desserts, Beverages)
* Designed menu cards with images, prices, and short descriptions
* Built an item customization popup (quantity, add-ons, calories, allergens)*
* Designed cart, checkout, and payment screens
* Added Dine-In table actions like call waiter, request water, reorder, and call for bill
* Ensured a clear separation between Dine-In and Takeaway flows

📘 What I learned:
* How to structure a real-world restaurant ordering flow
* The importance of decision points (Dine-In vs Takeaway, Login vs Guest)
* How clear information hierarchy improves menu browsing
* Why separating cart and payment reduces user anxiety
* How UX decisions directly impact speed, clarity, and user confidence

💡 Key Insight:
Designing for restaurants is not just about visuals — it’s about reducing waiting time, avoiding confusion, and giving users control without overwhelming them.
